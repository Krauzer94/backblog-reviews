## A Fitting Conclusion
The last title to the Arkham franchise is an outstanding sequel, serving as the final main installment in the critically acclaimed modern Batman videogame series. The game delivers an immersive Batman experience, allowing you to explore a massive Gotham City while facing some of the MC's most dangerous villains. With its dark atmosphere, compelling narrative, excellent gameplay mechanics, and to top it all off, with great visuals even for today's standards, it manages to capture the essence of what makes Batman such an iconic character. It is undoubtedly one of the best superhero games ever made. And while the previous titles were already considered incredible titles, Arkham Knight manages to surpass them in almost every way, especially when it comes to exploration and the open-world mechanics.

## Improved Mechanics
One of the game's strongest aspects is its gameplay, which builds upon and improves many of the mechanics introduced in its predecessors. The free-flow combat system feels just as good as its previous iterations, but it feels a little bit more fluid. Now, the combat-centered gadgets have a quick-action you can perform during combat, making them more enjoyable to use and to combo with since you can alternate between them on the fly. As for the traditional aspects of it, it still feels amazing to finish off a group of enemies, without being hit, so no changes here if you just want to punch and counter things. The stealth sections also received some improvements, with quite more options such as hacking drones, hopping inside underground ducts from above, and much more.

## A Massive Gotham
The open-world design is another major highlight, Gotham City is absolutely massive, featuring impressive architecture, detailed streets and towering skyscrapers. It is quite impressive, even today, on how much details absolutely all the environments have. Traversing the city using the grappling hook and gliding mechanics is still satisfying, even when the most effective way is by using the Batmobile, which is by far, my favorite aspect of this title. Its addition is one of the biggest changes compared to previous games, introducing new ways for exploration, puzzle solving, and especially in combat. The only downside of it that I can think of is that its combat can feel repetitive due to how often you find enemy vehicles to engage with. And while this trait is related to the main plot of the game, I still thin it should have been toned down a bit.

## A Darker Story
The story is engaging and provides a darker, more personal look into Batman's character. The main plot revolves around Scarecrow's plan to unleash fear throughout Gotham City, forcing a complete city evacuation while crime factions take advantage of the situation. At the same time, Batman must deal with the new villain, Arkham Knight, who has a personal connection to the MC's past. The story explores Batman's psychological struggles, fears, guilt, and determination, adding more depth to a character, especially when compared to the previous entries. Although some plot developments can be predictable, the overall experience remains compelling, with several memorable moments and a satisfying conclusion to the trilogy. About the only complaint I can think of is that some o the villains have way too little screen time, despite all of them having their own dedicated side-missions to complete.

## Outstanding Presentation
Another aspect worth highlighting is the game's presentation, even years after its original release, this game remains a visually impressive title. The character models are highly detailed, the lighting effects are excellent, and the rain-soaked streets create an incredibly cinematic atmosphere. The OST complements the darker tone of the game perfectly, while the sound design and voice acting further enhance the experience. There are no big surprises for those experienced with this franchise, but the level of quality is still worth mentioning since it was carried over up until the its very last iteration. Small details, animations, and environmental interactions also contribute to making Gotham feel like a believable living city.

## PC Performance Issues
Unfortunately, the PC version had a disastrous launch in 2015, becoming infamous for its severe performance issues, stuttering, crashes, and poor optimization. These problems were significant enough to result in the temporary suspension of PC sales shortly after release. Despite all that, I can safely say that these issues are long fixed, and it is a very safe recommendation for its state today, when it comes to the PC port. Nevertheless, when running properly, the game can still look and perform remarkably well, offering an experience that holds up surprisingly well compared to many more recent releases. Though there is still one big issue, and that is battery drain, if your device runs with a limited battery capacity, this title won't make it last much during a long session.

## Final Verdict
Overall, Arkham Knight is an exceptional game and a fitting conclusion to Arkham franchise. It takes the foundations established by its predecessors and expands upon them with a larger world, improved combat and more ambitious storytelling. While the Batmobile sections can occasionally become tiresome and the original PC release left a disappointing impression, these shortcomings do little to hurt the quality of the overall experience. Whether you are a longtime Batman fan or simply someone who enjoys open-world action games, this is a must-play out of all the Batman Akrham entries. My very last recommendation is to no try to 100% if. This is one of those titles which makes is a miserable experience if you try to do everything it has to offer, even for the most die-hard fans out there.
